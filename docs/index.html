Coin minus trust

The coin's fold change minus its trust's fold change, from the reference date on the overview tab (). Above zero, the coin on Coinbase outperformed the trust. Trading days only.

Why the gap moves Before January 2024 GBTC was a closed-end trust that traded at a premium and later at a deep discount to its bitcoin, which drives most of the early swings. Since its conversion to an ETF, the gap mostly reflects the 1.5% annual fee and the hours between the NYSE close and the Coinbase daily close.

Method

  • Returns: log returns on the dates both series have a close; weekly and monthly returns use the last matched close in each ISO week or calendar month.
  • Beta and alpha: ordinary least squares of y on x, with t intervals on n − 2 degrees of freedom. Alpha and mean returns are annualized by the observed number of periods per year in the window.
  • Correlation: Pearson r with a Fisher-z interval, as in R's cor.test.
  • Volatility: sample standard deviation × √(periods per year), with an exact χ² interval under normality.
  • Caveats: returns are close to serially uncorrelated but heavy-tailed and volatility-clustered, so the intervals assume more regularity than the data have. Treat them as descriptive.
  • Validation: tests/validate_stats.R checks the browser code against R's lm, confint, cor.test, t.test and qchisq. They agree to better than 1 part in 108.
